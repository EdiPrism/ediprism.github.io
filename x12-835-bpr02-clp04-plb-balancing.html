ASC X12 835 Electronic Remittance Advice Balancing (BPR-02, CLP-04 & PLB)

Understanding how check/EFT totals (BPR-02), claim-level payments (CLP-04), service lines (SVC-03), and provider-level withholdings/recoupments (PLB) reconcile inside an 835 ERA.

1. Transaction-Level Remittance Equation

BPR-02 (Total Actual Provider Payment) == SUM(CLP-04 Claim Payments) - SUM(PLB-04, PLB-06, PLB-08, PLB-10, PLB-12, PLB-14)

Notice the minus sign on PLB: positive PLB amounts decrease the net EFT/check payout (e.g., prior-overpayment recoupment WO), whereas negative PLB amounts increase the check payout (e.g., interest payment L6).

3. Diffing Resent or Adjusted 835 Files Without Envelope Noise

When comparing yesterday's 835 against a payer reissue, raw text diff flags every single ISA, GS, ST, and BPR-16 date/control number — even if only one CAS adjustment changed. In EdiPrism, press Alt+Shift+D (Semantic EDI Diff) to mask envelope noise and unwrap single-line streams automatically.
