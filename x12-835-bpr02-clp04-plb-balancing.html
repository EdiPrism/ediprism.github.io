ASC X12 835 ERA Remittance Balancing (BPR-02, CLP-04, CAS & PLB)

Why does BPR-02 frequently disagree with a naive SUM(CLP-04) SQL query? Because provider-level PLB withholdings, recoupments, and interest use inverted accounting signs.

The Three Mandatory 835 Balancing Equations

1. Service Line (Loop 2110):  SVC-02 (Billed) - Σ CAS(line adjustments) == SVC-03 (Line Paid)
2. Claim Level  (Loop 2100):  CLP-03 (Billed) - Σ CAS(claim adjustments) == CLP-04 (Claim Paid)
3. Check / EFT  (Header):     BPR-02 (Net Payment) == Σ CLP-04 - Σ PLB(adjustments)
Critical PLB Sign Rule In the PLB segment (PLB-04, PLB-06, PLB-08...), a positive amount (e.g. WO:REC99*125.00 overpayment recovery) reduces the check/EFT payment (BPR-02), whereas a negative amount (e.g. L6:INT*-4.50 prompt-pay interest) increases BPR-02!

Frequently Asked Engineering Questions

Does CLP-05 Patient Responsibility subtract from CLP-04?

No. Patient responsibility amounts (reported in CAS*PR segments and summarized in CLP-05) are already part of the CAS adjustments subtracted from CLP-03 to arrive at CLP-04. Subtracting CLP-05 a second time double-counts patient deductibles/copays.
