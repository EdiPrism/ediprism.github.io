Frequently Asked Engineering Questions

Should our retry queue resubmit AAA-03 = 75 automatically?

No. Only transient infrastructure codes like AAA-03 = 42 paired with AAA-04 = R (Resubmit) should be retried automatically. Demographic mismatches (71, 72, 73, 75) carry AAA-04 = C and require correcting member input first.
