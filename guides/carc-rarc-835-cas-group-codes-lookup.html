Frequently Asked Engineering Questions

How many adjustment triplets can a single CAS segment hold?

Up to 6 triplets per CAS segment: after CAS-01 Group Code, one CAS segment can carry Reason/Amount/Quantity at elements (02..04), (05..07), (08..10), (11..13), (14..16), and (17..19). Parsers that only read CAS-02/03 miss adjustments 2 through 6!
