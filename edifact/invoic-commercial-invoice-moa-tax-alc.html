UN/EDIFACT INVOIC Commercial Invoice & VAT Reconciliation Guide

European and APAC e-invoicing pipelines require strict decimal reconciliation across line-item net amounts (MOA+203), allowances (ALC), taxable bases (MOA+125), VAT (MOA+176), and total payable (MOA+77).

Essential MOA Monetary Qualifiers & Balancing Formula

QualifierLocationAccounting Meaning & Equation
MOA+203SG26 LineLine item net amount (QTY+47 * PRI+AAA minus line ALC allowances).
MOA+79SummaryTotal line items amount == Σ MOA+203.
MOA+125SummaryTaxable amount (MOA+79 adjusted by header-level ALC discounts/charges).
MOA+176SummaryMessage total duty/tax/fee amount (sum of SG52 TAX/MOA+124 VAT buckets).
MOA+77 / MOA+86SummaryInvoice Total Payable == MOA+125 + MOA+176.

Frequently Asked Engineering Questions

How are Credit Notes represented in UN/EDIFACT INVOIC?

Instead of negative quantities, standard UN/EDIFACT Credit Notes use BGM+381 (Credit Note) with positive quantities and monetary amounts, referencing the original invoice via SG1 RFF+IV.
