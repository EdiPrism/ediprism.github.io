UN/EDIFACT ORDERS Purchase Order Segment Group Reference (D.96A / D.01B)

ORDERS is the global retail, FMCG, and manufacturing equivalent of ASC X12 850, using explicit UNS+S section dividers to separate detail lines from trailer totals.

Frequently Asked Engineering Questions

Why is UNS+S mandatory in UN/EDIFACT ORDERS and INVOIC?

Because MOA and CNT segments can appear both inside line-item groups (SG25/SG26) and in the document summary, ISO 9735 grammar requires UNS+S to unambiguously transition the state machine from the detail section to the summary section.
