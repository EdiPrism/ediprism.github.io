UN/EDIFACT Freight Forwarding (IFTMIN/IFTSTA) & Automotive JIT (DELFOR/DELJIT)

Global freight forwarders, ocean carriers, and European Tier-1 automotive suppliers rely heavily on four specialized UN/EDIFACT message families.

Freight & Automotive Message Architecture Matrix

MessageDomainAnchor Segments & Hierarchy
IFTMINTransport InstructionShipper-to-carrier booking/waybill instruction: TSR service reqs, TDT+20 main carriage vessel/truck, LOC ports, GID goods items, EQD+CN ISO container numbers.
IFTSTAMultimodal StatusCarrier milestone tracking (equivalent to X12 214): CNI consignment, STS status event (Picked Up, Gate-In, Vessel Departed, Customs Cleared, Delivered).
DELFORDelivery ScheduleAutomotive rolling forecast (equivalent to X12 830): GIS/NAD plant dock, LIN part #, SCC commitment level (1=Firm, 4=Planning), QTY+113 + DTM buckets.
DELJITJust-In-Time Call-OffSequenced assembly-line call-off (equivalent to X12 862): SEQ production sequence number, exact dock arrival window DTM, and KANBAN card RFF+AAK.
