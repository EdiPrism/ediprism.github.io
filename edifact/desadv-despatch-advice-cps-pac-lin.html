100% Offline Reference EDIFACT DESADV

UN/EDIFACT DESADV Despatch Advice / ASN Packaging Hierarchy

Where ASC X12 856 uses HL segments to model pallets and cartons, UN/EDIFACT DESADV uses Segment Group 10 CPS (Consignment Packing Sequence) parent-child pointers.

How CPS Models Shipment → Pallet → Carton → LIN

BGM+351+ASN-2026-4401+9'
DTM+137:20261114:102'
DTM+11:20261114:102'                         <-- 11 = Despatch Date
NAD+BY+4012345000018::9'
NAD+DP+4012345000056::9'
CPS+1'                                       <-- CPS-01 = 1 (Top Shipment Level, no parent)
CPS+2+1+3'                                   <-- CPS-01 = 2, Parent CPS-02 = 1, 3 = Outer Pallet
PAC+1++201'                                  <-- 1 Euro Pallet (ISO 201)
PCI+33E'                                     <-- Marked with Serial Shipping Container Code
GIN+BJ+354123450000000129'                   <-- BJ = 18-digit SSCC-18 Pallet Barcode
LIN+1++5412345111220:EN'                     <-- SG17 Item packed on Pallet #2
QTY+12:40:CT'                                <-- 12 = Despatch Quantity
RFF+ON:PO-EU-77412'                          <-- Original Purchase Order Reference

Frequently Asked Engineering Questions

What do CPS-03 Packaging Level codes 1, 3, and 4 mean?

In EANCOM/UNECE DESADV profiles, CPS-03 = 1 denotes Inner packaging, 3 denotes Outer/Transport packaging (such as a shrink-wrapped pallet), and 4 denotes No packaging hierarchy.
