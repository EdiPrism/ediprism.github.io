UN/EDIFACT ORDRSP Purchase Order Response & Line Status Codes

ORDRSP is the UN/EDIFACT counterpart to X12 855, communicating full order acceptance, partial quantity/delivery-date changes, or line rejections.

Header BGM-03 & Line LIN-02 Action Codes

CoordinateCodeMeaning
BGM-0329Accepted without amendment (entire ORDERS accepted as sent).
BGM-034Change (one or more SG26 LIN lines amended in qty, price, or DTM date).
BGM-0327Not accepted (entire purchase order rejected).
LIN-025 / 3 / 75 = Accepted without amendment; 3 = Changed (check QTY+113 / QVR variance); 7 = Not accepted.

Frequently Asked Engineering Questions

What does QVR report inside SG26 of an ORDRSP?

QVR (Quantity Variances) explicitly states the difference between the buyer's ordered quantity and the supplier's confirmed quantity along with a discrepancy reason code (such as backorder or minimum order quantity).
