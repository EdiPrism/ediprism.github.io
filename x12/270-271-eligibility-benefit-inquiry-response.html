Frequently Asked Engineering Questions

Why does Loop 2120C/D have LS*2120 and LE*2120 wrapper segments?

In X12 271, Loop 2120 (Benefit Related Entity, such as an assigned Primary Care Physician or third-party dental/pharmacy administrator) is nested inside Loop 2110 EB using explicit LS*2120 (Loop Header) and LE*2120 (Loop Trailer) bounded loop markers to prevent parser ambiguity.
