ASC X12 834 Benefit Enrollment & Maintenance Structure (005010X220A1)

Unlike 837 and 270, the 834 transaction does not use HL segments — instead, each repeating INS segment opens a flat Loop 2000 member record for either a subscriber (INS-01=Y) or a dependent (INS-01=N).

Audit Files vs. Change Files (BGN-08) & Loop 2000 Anatomy

ST*834*0001*005010X220A1~
BGN*00*ROSTER20261112*20261112*0800****2~    <-- BGN-08 = 2 (Full File Audit) vs 4 (Changes Only)
N1*P5*ACME CORP SPONSOR*FI*369988776~        <-- Loop 1000A Plan Sponsor
N1*IN*APEX HEALTH CARRIER*FI*361122334~      <-- Loop 1000B Payer / Insurer
INS*Y*18*030*XN*A***FT~                      <-- Loop 2000 Subscriber (18=Self, 030=Audit/Compare, FT=Full-Time)
REF*0F*SUB884920~                            <-- Subscriber Number (links family members!)
DTP*336*D8*20240115~                         <-- Employment Begin Date
NM1*IL*1*MORRIS*ELENA****34*123456789~       <-- Loop 2100A Member Name + SSN (Scrubbed by EdiPrism!)

Frequently Asked Engineering Questions

How are dependents linked to their subscriber in X12 834 without HL segments?

Every member record (both subscriber INS*Y*18 and dependent INS*N*01/19) carries Loop 2000 REF*0F containing the shared Subscriber Number, allowing enrollment engines and EdiPrism to group households deterministically.
