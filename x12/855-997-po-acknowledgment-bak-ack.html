ASC X12 855 PO Acknowledgment (BAK / ACK) & 3PL Warehouse Flows

While a 997/999 only confirms X12 syntax receipt, an 855 communicates commercial seller commitment: accepted as-is, backordered, price/date amended, or rejected.

High-Frequency BAK-02 Header & ACK-01 Line Codes

CoordinateCodeCommercial Meaning
BAK-02AD / AC / RDAD = Accepted with No Detail Change; AC = Acknowledge With Detail & Change; RD = Reject with Detail.
ACK-01IAItem Accepted (ships as ordered).
ACK-01IB / ICIB = Item Backordered (check ACK-04/05 estimated ship date); IC = Item Accepted — Changes Made.
ACK-01IQ / IP / IRIQ = Quantity Changed; IP = Price Changed; IR = Item Rejected.

Frequently Asked Engineering Questions

How do 940 and 945 fit alongside 850, 855, and 856?

When a brand uses a third-party logistics (3PL) warehouse, the ERP converts an accepted 850 into a 940 Warehouse Shipping Order sent to the 3PL; once the 3PL ships the parcel/LTL pallet, it returns a 945 Warehouse Shipping Advice, which the ERP translates into the customer-facing 856 ASN and 810 Invoice.
