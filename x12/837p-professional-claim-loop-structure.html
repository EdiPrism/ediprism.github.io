Frequently Asked Engineering Questions

Why do payers reject 837P when a PO Box is placed in Loop 2010AA?

Under 005010X222A1 rules, Loop 2010AA N3/N4 must contain the Billing Provider's physical street address and 9-digit ZIP code. Lockbox or PO Box remittance addresses belong exclusively in Loop 2010AB (NM1*87 Pay-To Address).

How do SV1-07 diagnosis pointers reference the HI segment?

SV1-07 contains 1-based ordinal positions (1 through 12, delimited by :) pointing to the diagnosis composites declared in Loop 2300 HI (where HI-01 is pointer 1, HI-02 is pointer 2, etc.).
