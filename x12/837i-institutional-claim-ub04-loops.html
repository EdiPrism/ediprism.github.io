ASC X12 837I Institutional Claim Structure (005010X223A2)

Used by hospitals, skilled nursing facilities, and ambulatory surgical centers, 837I differs sharply from 837P in Type-of-Bill encoding, HI code qualifiers, and SV2-03 line-charge balancing.

Critical Structural Differences Between 837I and 837P

Feature837P Professional (X222A1)837I Institutional (X223A2)
Paper Form EquivalentCMS-1500UB-04 (CMS-1450)
CLM-05 Composite2-digit Place of Service + B + Frequency (11:B:1)Facility Type + Bill Classification + A + Frequency (e.g. 11:A:1 = Hospital Inpatient Admit-Through-Discharge)
Institutional Claim CodeNot usedMandatory CL1 segment (Admission Type, Source, Patient Discharge Status)

Frequently Asked Engineering Questions

Does SV2 require Revenue Code 0001 Total Charge in 5010 837I?

No. Unlike paper UB-04 forms where FL42 line 23 carries Revenue Code 0001 for total charges, electronic 5010 837I prohibits Revenue Code 0001 in Loop 2400 SV2 because CLM-02 already carries the total claim charge.
