ASC X12 856 Advance Ship Notice (S → O → T → P → I HL Tree)

Retail chargebacks on 856 ASNs almost always stem from inverted HL packaging trees (SOPI vs. SOIP) or orphan HL-02 pallet/carton parent IDs.

Frequently Asked Engineering Questions

What does CTT-01 count in an 856 ASN?

In an X12 856 transaction, CTT-01 equals the total number of HL segments across the entire transaction set (not just Item-level HLs).
