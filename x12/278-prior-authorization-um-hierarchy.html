Frequently Asked Engineering Questions

Where is the approved Prior Authorization Reference Number returned in a 278 response?

When HCR-01 indicates certification (such as A1 Certified in total), HCR-02 carries the Review Identification Number (Prior Auth Number), which is subsequently submitted on the outbound 837 claim in Loop 2300 REF*G1.
