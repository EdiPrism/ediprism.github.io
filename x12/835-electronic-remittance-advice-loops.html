ASC X12 835 ERA Payment & Remittance Loop Reference (005010X221A1)

835 transmits both Electronic Funds Transfer (EFT) payment instructions and detailed Explanation of Benefits (EOB) claim/service adjudication back to healthcare providers.

Complete 835 Loop Hierarchy (1000A/B → 2000 → 2100 → 2110)

Section / LoopAnchor SegmentContents & Reconciliation Role
HeaderBPR + TRNBPR-01 Handling Code (I=Remit+Pay, H=Notification Only), BPR-02 Total Actual Provider Payment, TRN-02 Check/EFT Trace #.

2100CLPClaim Payment Information: CLP-01 Patient Control #, CLP-02 Status (1=Primary, 2=Secondary, 4=Denied, 22=Reversal), CLP-03 Billed, CLP-04 Paid, CLP-05 Patient Resp, CLP-07 Payer ICN.

SummaryPLBProvider-Level Adjustments (overpayment recoupments WO, forwarding balances FB, interest L6, IRS levy WU).

Handling CLP-02 = 22 Reversals & Negative Payments

When a payer retracts a previously paid claim, the 835 contains a reversal CLP with CLP-02 = 22 (Reversal of Previous Payment) where CLP-03 and CLP-04 carry negative monetary values (e.g. CLP*PAT01*22*-340.50*-280.00*...~), often followed immediately by a corrected replacement CLP (CLP-02 = 1).

Frequently Asked Engineering Questions

What does BPR-01 = H mean in an 835 file?

BPR-01 = H indicates Notification Only (no actual funds move, so BPR-02 MUST equal 0). This commonly occurs when all claims in the remittance were applied to deductible or denied, or when PLB recoupments offset 100% of claim payments.
