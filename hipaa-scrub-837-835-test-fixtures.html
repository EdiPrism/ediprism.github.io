Frequently Asked Engineering Questions

Why does replacing NM1-09 with 9999999999 fail X12 5010 validation?

When NM1-08 equals XX (CMS National Provider Identifier), clearinghouses validate NM1-09 against the ISO/IEC 7812 Luhn Mod-10 check digit prefixed with 80840. For 999999999, the valid 10th check digit is 5 (9999999995), so 9999999999 fails with IK4-03 = I12.
