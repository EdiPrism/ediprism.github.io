Fixing ISA Segment Must Be 106 Characters (Exact Byte-Offset Map 0..105)

Strict X12 parsers like Java StAEDI, Python pyx12, BizTalk, and clearinghouse gateways read delimiters from hard-coded byte indices 3, 104, and 105 — causing instant crashes whenever chat tools collapse whitespace inside ISA-02, ISA-04, ISA-06, or ISA-08.

Frequently Asked Engineering Questions

Do CRLF line breaks after ~ affect the 106-byte ISA rule?

No. The 106-byte count runs from 'I' (byte 0) through the segment terminator character '~' (byte 105). Optional CR or LF formatting characters after byte 105 follow the segment terminator, though production VAN streams typically omit line breaks altogether.
