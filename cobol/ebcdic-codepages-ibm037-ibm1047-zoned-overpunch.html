IBM037 vs. IBM1047 EBCDIC Codepages & Signed Zoned Decimal Overpunch

When a COBOL numeric field omits COMP-3 and SIGN IS SEPARATE (PIC S9(5)V99 DISPLAY), the algebraic sign is punched into the upper 4-bit zone nibble of the final byte, rendering 125.0} or 125.0{ in naive string decoders.

IBM037 vs. IBM1047 vs. IBM01140 Selection Guide

  • IBM037: US / Canada z/OS MVS batch, CICS, DB2, and US banking/card-network interchange standard.
  • IBM1047: z/OS UNIX System Services (`USS`) and C compiler codepage (swaps `^`, `[`, `]`, `~` code points relative to `IBM037`).
  • IBM01140: Identical to `IBM037` except `0x9F` maps to the Euro currency sign (`EUR`) instead of the international currency symbol.

Frequently Asked Engineering Questions

What does '000452N' mean in a COBOL PIC S9(5)V99 field?

In IBM037 EBCDIC, 'N' is hex 0xD5: zone 0xD (negative) + digit 5, so '000452N' with V99 scale decodes to exact BigDecimal -45.25.
