How IK3-02 Segment Position Counting Actually Works

Why Line Numbers Never Match IK3-02 IK3-02 is a 1-based segment count starting at ST == 1 inside the specific transaction set matched by AK2-02 — ignoring ISA, GS, and every preceding ST..SE block in the file. Plus, single-line production files sit entirely on Line 1. 
ISA*00*...~                                    (Ignored by IK3-02)
GS*HC*...~                                     (Ignored by IK3-02)
ST*837*0001*005010X222A1~                      <-- Position 1
BHT*0019*00*BATCH92*20261112*1430*CH~          <-- Position 2
NM1*41*2*SUBMITTER*****46*SUB01~               <-- Position 3
PER*IC*EDI DESK*TE*8005550199~                 <-- Position 4
NM1*40*2*PAYER*****46*PAY01~                   <-- Position 5
HL*1**20*1~                                    <-- Position 6
NM1*85*2*CLINIC*****XX*1234567893~             <-- Position 7
HL*2*1*22*0~                                   <-- Position 8
SBR*P*18*******CI~                             <-- Position 9
NM1*IL*1*DOE*JANE****MI*MEM99102~              <-- Position 10
CLM*PAT01*340.50***11:B:1*Y*A*Y*Y~             <-- Position 11 (IK3*CLM*11*2300*8~ / IK4*5:1*1331*7*99~)

Frequently Asked Engineering Questions

What does CTX*SITUATIONAL TRIGGER mean in an X12 999 acknowledgment?

When IK3-04 or IK4-03 reports an implementation situational error (such as I6, I9, or I10), the clearinghouse emits a child CTX segment identifying the exact companion-guide segment and element whose value triggered that situational requirement.

How do composite positions in IK4-01 work?

IK4-01 uses a composite structure: IK4-01-1 is the 1-based element position within the segment, and IK4-01-2 is the 1-based component position inside a composite field. For example, IK4*5:2 points to element 05, subfield 2 (such as CLM-05-2).
