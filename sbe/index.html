Frequently Asked Engineering Questions

Does SbePrism hijack normal pom.xml, logback.xml, or Android XML files?

Never. SbePrism inspects only the first 4 KB of .xml files for <messageSchema / <sbe:messageSchema markers, leaving all standard XML files 100% untouched.

Does SbePrism require WinPcap, Npcap, or native libpcap DLLs to open .pcap files?

No. SbePrism includes a 100% pure-JVM microsecond and hardware-nanosecond .pcap / .pcapng Ethernet/VLAN/IPv4/IPv6/UDP/TCP payload extractor that runs cross-platform with zero native drivers.
