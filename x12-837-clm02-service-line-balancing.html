ASC X12 837 Financial Balancing (CLM-02) & HL Hierarchy Validation

Two of the most frequent clearinghouse front-end rejections in 005010X222A1 (837P), 005010X223A2 (837I), and 005010X224A2 (837D) are service-line charge imbalances and broken HL parent pointers.

1. Claim Charge Balancing Formula

Every Loop 2300 CLM-02 (Total Claim Charge Amount) must equal the exact decimal sum of its child Loop 2400 service lines:

837 Transaction TypeClaim Header TotalService Line Charge ElementBalancing Equation
837P ProfessionalCLM-02SV1-02CLM-02 == Σ SV1-02
837I InstitutionalCLM-02SV2-03CLM-02 == Σ SV2-03
837D DentalCLM-02SV3-02CLM-02 == Σ SV3-02

Always use exact base-10 BigDecimal arithmetic when generating or validating 837 claims — IEEE-754 floating-point math produces $0.01 rounding drift on multi-line institutional claims.

2. HL Parent-Child Hierarchy Rules (2000A → 2000B → 2000C)

  • HL-01 (Hierarchical ID Number) must be unique and increment sequentially starting from 1 within each ST..SE set.
  • HL-02 (Parent ID) must be blank on root Billing Provider nodes (HL-03 == 20) and must reference an already-seen HL-01 on Subscriber (22) and Patient (23) nodes.
  • Trimming a multi-subscriber production 837 file in a text editor without re-numbering HL-01/HL-02 creates immediate orphan HL rejections.
